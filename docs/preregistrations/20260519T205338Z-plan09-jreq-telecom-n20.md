# Pre-Registration: Plan 09 J/req Cell — τ²-Bench Telecom N=20 (2026-05-19)

**Authored: 2026-05-19T20:53:38Z**
**Scope: descriptive single-cell paired J/req measurement; corrective replication of the telecom N=5 cell that fell short of the sampling floor on the flat-http arm.**

This is a **corrective** mechanical replication that applies the
methodology lesson from the N=5 cell
([claim](../../.apxm/docs/claims/plan09-jreq-telecom-n5.md),
pre-reg `852a8c75`): short-wall, naturally-parallel τ²-bench
domains need higher N to clear the ≥30-sample integration floor.
Bumping N=5 → N=20 on the same domain/seed scales the wall window
~4×, projected to ~240-300s per arm with ≥120 rocm-smi samples per
arm.

## Why N=20, not `--max-concurrency 1`

Both fix the sampling floor; N=20 also lands on a design point we
already shipped a separate quality claim against
([`agentic-accuracy-telecom-n20-powered.md`](../../.apxm/docs/claims/agentic-accuracy-telecom-n20-powered.md):
paired ratio 1.226 CI [0.992, 1.532], indeterminate at HIGH
boundary), so any cross-cell wall-time comparison is at least
nominally meaningful. The N=20 quality finding was later
direction-flipped at N=50 → demoted to within-variance noise per
[`agentic-accuracy-telecom-n50-escalation.md`](../../.apxm/docs/claims/agentic-accuracy-telecom-n50-escalation.md).
This J/req cell does not re-open the quality question; it ships
power data only.

## What gets measured

1. **Per-arm joules consumed**: rocm-smi sidecar samples
   `total_watts` every 2s. Trapezoidal integration → arm-aggregate
   joules.
2. **Tasks completed per arm**: tau2 reports N=20 paired tasks.
3. **J/req per arm**: joules ÷ 20 tasks.
4. **Wall window per arm**: seconds between sidecar start and stop.

## What gets reported

Single CSV row + JSON record per arm:

| arm | n_tasks | total_joules | joules_per_task | mean_watts | sample_count | window_seconds |

Plus the **descriptive ratio** `apxm-on.J / flat-http.J`.

## What is NOT being claimed

- **Not claiming "APXM is more/less energy-efficient than flat-HTTP".**
  Single trace per arm = descriptive only.
- **Not claiming any quality result.** Pass-at-1 reproducibility is
  expected within service-drift; not re-claimed.
- **Not re-opening the telecom regression question.** Already
  closed by the N=50 escalation arc.

## Honest negatives anticipated

- **Idle power dominates ~50% of the per-sample budget** at this
  service config — same shape as the N=5 cells.
- **The two arms run sequentially**, prefix-cache state may differ.
- **N=20 is still single-trial-per-task** on the J/req axis: 20
  paired tasks but 1 power trace per arm.

## Stop conditions

- If the service becomes unhealthy mid-run: kill HAL, stop sidecar,
  report partial result.
- If sidecar samples < 30 per arm: this corrective replication
  ALSO fails the sampling floor → methodology requires further
  tuning (different domain choice, longer cooldown sample, etc.).
  Ship descriptively as a methodology note, do not promote.
- If sidecar samples ≥ 60 per arm (= ≥120s of capture, the natural
  target for N=20): **promote into the INT-pack J/req column as
  the second load-bearing methodology cell.**

## Why this is the right next J/req cell

The retail N=5 demo (76 / 75 samples) proved the pipeline. The
telecom N=5 mechanical replication failed the floor on one arm.
This cell either (a) confirms the pipeline scales to a different
domain with a longer-wall N (promotable second cell, methodology
proven across domains) or (b) reveals a deeper methodology issue
(unlikely given the retail demo's clean numbers, but worth knowing).
Either outcome is informative.
