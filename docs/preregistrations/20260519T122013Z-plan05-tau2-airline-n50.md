## Workload and rationale

- Benchmark: `tau2-bench` (vendored at `tools/external/tau2-bench/`,
  pin sha `0ed8c0ef0a1f6b024a0fb733e186922411874879`).
- Domain: `airline` — the **full 50-task suite** of the vendored
  τ²-bench airline domain (`tools/external/tau2-bench/data/tau2/domains/airline/tasks.json`
  has exactly 50 tasks). Running the full suite exhausts the
  available power on this domain without resorting to bootstrap or
  cross-task aggregation tricks.
- One trial per task (`--num-trials 1`). The pair is therefore 50
  paired binary observations — the maximum statistical separation
  achievable from this domain with the vendored task list.
- This run is the **escalation mandated by the N=20 cell's decision
  rule** ([`agentic-accuracy-airline-n20.md`](../../.apxm/docs/claims/agentic-accuracy-airline-n20.md)):
  the N=20 paired-difference CI was [−0.350, +0.150], spanning zero
  and extending slightly below the ±0.20 equivalence floor on the
  unfavorable side. The N=20 cell explicitly flagged itself as
  indeterminate and pointed to this N=50 cell as the next required
  step.

## Hypothesis under test

- Form (re-stated for the maximum-power-on-airline regime): At N=50
  airline tasks with the same seed in both arms, the paired
  difference apxm-on `pass_at_1` − flat-http `pass_at_1` falls into
  exactly one of three claim-bearing outcomes:
  1. **Equivalence claim**: 95% bootstrap CI on the paired diff is
     **fully inside** the pre-registered ±0.20 equivalence band.
     Allowed claim: "APXM-on does not change τ²-bench airline
     pass_at_1 vs flat-http at the maximum-power-on-airline regime."
  2. **Superiority claim**: lower CI bound > 0. Allowed claim:
     "APXM-on raises τ²-bench airline pass_at_1 by [lower, upper] vs
     flat-http."
  3. **Honest-negative claim**: upper CI bound < 0. Allowed claim:
     "APXM-on degrades τ²-bench airline pass_at_1 by [lower, upper]
     vs flat-http; the regression is consistent with the Plan 04
     ShareGPT short-multi-turn finding and opens a Plan 08 follow-up
     ticket."
- If the CI still spans zero AND extends outside ±0.20 (still
  indeterminate at N=50): ship as a **final airline indeterminate
  claim**, explicitly stating that the airline domain at this
  model + image + service configuration cannot resolve the
  apxm-on vs flat-http question at the available sample size and
  that any further movement requires either a different model, a
  different domain, or per-task variance reduction (multi-trial
  averaging).
- Decision rule is fully enumerated above — no post-hoc choice of
  decision boundary is allowed.

## Metric tier

- Primary metric: `pass_at_1` per arm at N=50, plus the **paired
  difference** apxm-on − flat-http on identical task IDs.
- Secondary metrics: `mean_steps`, `mean_tool_calls`,
  `mean_duration_s`, `db_match_rate`, `termination_reasons`,
  `agent_cost`, `user_cost`. At N=50 these become defensible
  supporting evidence (still not the primary metric).
- Cost note: tau2's `agent_cost` / `user_cost` columns are populated
  from LiteLLM's token-pricing tables. Because the underlying model
  is local (vLLM `gpt-oss-120b`) with no associated price, the cost
  columns are expected to be near-zero across both arms — they are
  recorded for completeness, not interpreted.
